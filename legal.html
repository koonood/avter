What after. offers

after. creates a free, statistics-based conversation report and an optional one-time AI report for a selected date range.

Online payment is not enabled in this version. The page describes the service and pricing; no charge is made here.

Products and service

Free conversation reportA statistical report generated from the uploaded conversation archive. It includes communication volume, activity over time and other measurable patterns.
One-time AI reportAn optional written analysis of messages in the date range selected by the customer. The exact selected range and message count determine the quoted price.

The report describes message patterns and is not therapy, a diagnosis, legal advice or a determination of anyone’s private feelings.

Prices

Free report: 0. AI report: one-time price, calculated from the number of messages in the selected period. The current quote is shown before a customer starts the AI analysis.

Digital delivery

There is no physical product or shipping. The free report is displayed in the browser after the archive is processed. When payment is enabled, the AI report will be made available in the browser for the selected archive period after successful payment and processing. A payment receipt will be issued through the payment provider and applicable seller process.

At present, payment processing is not connected in this site build; the AI analysis control starts analysis directly and does not charge a customer.

Terms of service, order and refunds

Before any paid order is enabled, the customer should be shown the exact service, selected date range, message count, total price and seller details, and should explicitly confirm the order. The service is delivered electronically in the browser. No recurring subscription is offered by this price list.

For an order that has not started, or if delivery fails, contact the seller using the details below. Refunds, cancellation rights and exceptions will be handled under the mandatory consumer rules applicable to the seller and customer. The seller’s legal identity and support contact must be completed before accepting paid orders.

Privacy and message archives

Conversation exports can contain sensitive personal information. The app processes an uploaded archive on its server to build the report. In this version, parsed messages and analysis jobs are kept in application memory while the server is running; the upload flow does not save the archive as a permanent file. For an AI report, messages from the selected date range are sent to OpenAI’s API for analysis. OpenAI’s processing is governed by its applicable API terms and privacy documentation.

Upload only conversations you are entitled to use and tell the other participants where required by law. Do not upload content if you do not agree to this processing. A complete privacy notice, including the responsible data controller, lawful basis, retention period, user rights and contact for privacy requests, must be completed for the seller’s jurisdiction before launch.
